# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences in roughly equal measure:

- **Clients**: founders and business owners, mostly in MENA (Syria, KSA), who need a production website or web system built, often bilingual. They arrive from LinkedIn or referrals and want to know quickly whether Karim can own a build end to end. Success means a Start a Project submission.
- **Employers**: hiring managers and recruiters for team-lead or senior full-stack roles. They skim for proof of ownership, scope and seniority. Success means a LinkedIn message or an interview.

Both read on phones as often as on desktops, frequently between other tasks.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio that proves Karim Alrajjal leads and ships production web systems from architecture to launch. It does this through real shipped client work (13 case studies), measurable impact and a clear path to start a project. It exists to turn a skim into trust, and trust into a conversation.

## Brand Personality

**Precise, calm, owned.** The voice of an engineer who ships and takes responsibility for the result. Quiet confidence, no hype, no buzzwords. Statements over adjectives, evidence over claims. The emotional goal is reassurance: "this person will handle it."

## Anti-references

- **Agency flash**: scroll-jacking, long intros, motion for its own sake, content held hostage by animation.
- **Generic WordPress**: stock-theme look, busy sidebars, carousel sliders, interchangeable sections.
- Also avoid the SaaS template: hero-metric stat strips, identical icon-card grids, gradient glows.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof before polish.** Real shipped work, real outcomes, real screenshots carry the page. Decoration never competes with evidence.
2. **Practice what you preach.** A site selling performance, SEO and accessibility has to be fast, crawlable and accessible itself.
3. **Calm confidence.** Restraint in motion and color. One deliberate moment per screen, not a show.
4. **One clear next step.** Every page leads to a single primary action: start a project (clients) or get in touch (employers).
5. **Content renders without permission.** Nothing important waits on JavaScript or an animation to become visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast and 3:1 for non-text UI such as form borders; full keyboard support with visible focus; a proper heading hierarchy (one h1 per page); announced form errors and status; `prefers-reduced-motion` fully respected. The audience is bilingual (Arabic/English), so case-study names and copy must handle mixed scripts gracefully.
